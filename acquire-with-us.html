Work With Us

A Thoughtful Transition for the Business You Built.

M.J.E.R.D. evaluates opportunities to acquire and operate established businesses for the long term. We aim to support continuity for customers and employees, respect the owner’s legacy, and pursue a practical transition process.

Important: Every potential transaction remains subject to diligence, financing, negotiation, and definitive agreements. M.J.E.R.D. does not guarantee a transaction, confidentiality beyond what actual procedures support, job retention, seller financing, or a specific closing timeline. No acquisition has closed to date.

🏢

Business Owners

If you own a business and are considering a transition, retirement, or sale, M.J.E.R.D. Enterprise offers a thoughtful path forward grounded in respect for what you have built.

We work directly with owners to understand your business, your goals, and your timeline before pursuing any transaction structure.

  • Confidential initial conversations
  • Flexible transaction structures may be considered
  • Seller transition planning
  • Long-term ownership approach
  • Every potential transaction remains subject to diligence, financing, negotiation, and definitive agreements

Strategic Partners

We collaborate with operators, industry specialists, and strategic partners who bring domain expertise to potential acquisitions.

If you have deep experience in a particular industry and are interested in a potential partnership or co-evaluation structure, we welcome a conversation.

Referral Sources

Business brokers, M&A advisors, accountants, and attorneys are often the first to know when a business owner is considering an exit.

If you regularly encounter business owners considering a sale, we welcome a conversation about potential referral relationships.

Capital Partners

As M.J.E.R.D. develops its acquisition pipeline, we evaluate relationships with capital partners who share our discipline around long-term value creation.

We prioritize alignment of incentives and time horizon above all else. All capital relationships are governed by applicable securities laws and regulations.
