Our Story

Built to Last.

M.J.E.R.D. Enterprise, Inc. is a Delaware corporation headquartered in Boynton Beach, Florida, evaluating established businesses for potential long-term ownership.

Why We Exist

Ownership Creates Legacy

M.J.E.R.D. Enterprise, Inc. was founded on the conviction that disciplined acquisition of fundamentally sound businesses—held for the long term—creates durable value for owners, employees, and communities alike.

Our approach is straightforward: evaluate businesses we understand, pursue transactions on practical terms, and operate what we own with long-term stewardship as the guiding principle.

We are not building a portfolio to flip. We are building a holding company designed to outlast its founder, operated with discipline whether markets are favorable or difficult.

Current Status: M.J.E.R.D. is an emerging company. No acquisition has closed to date. All potential opportunities remain subject to due diligence, financing availability, and definitive agreements.
