Company Overview

About M.J.E.R.D. Enterprise

M.J.E.R.D. Enterprise, Inc. is a Delaware corporation headquartered in Boynton Beach, Florida. The company evaluates established operating businesses for potential long-term ownership, subject to diligence, financing, and definitive agreements.

The company is in an early acquisition and capital formation stage. No acquisition has closed to date.
