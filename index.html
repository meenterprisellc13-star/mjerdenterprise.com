The M.J.E.R.D. Model

How We Approach Value Creation

A disciplined four-step process guiding how M.J.E.R.D. evaluates, pursues, and operates potential acquisitions.

01
Evaluate

Source established operating businesses with durable customer relationships, understandable economics, and long-term improvement potential.

02
Diligence

Conduct thorough, independent due diligence on operations, financials, and management before pursuing any transaction.

03
Acquire

Negotiate practical, disciplined transaction structures with clear terms, subject to definitive documentation and closing conditions.

04
Steward

Operate acquired businesses with long-term orientation, supporting continuity for customers and employees while improving operations.

Quality Businesses
